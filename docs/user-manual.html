1. Introduction

Scientech-IMS is used to manage inventory, stock movement, equipment, instruments, consumables, accessories, calibration, maintenance, transfers, and audit documents.

The system is designed so that daily users can enter stock and movement details, while managers and admins can approve requests, review alerts, download registers, and track audit history.

Main idea: The system stores business records permanently, such as products, stock, transactions, transfer history, PI details, DC details, calibration, and maintenance. Generated files such as monthly ZIP copies can be temporary, but the source data remains in the system.

For Store Users

Receive stock, issue stock, check stock balance, and respond to low-stock situations.

For Site Users

Request material, confirm transfer receipt, and track assigned equipment.

For Managers

Approve transfers, review compliance blocks, and monitor site-wise stock.

For Admin

Manage users, categories, locations, monthly documents, alerts, and audit data.

2. Basic Terms

New users should understand these terms before using the system.

Term Meaning Example
Product An item master record. It defines what the item is. DPT Chemical, Film Box, Ultrasonic Flaw Detector
Category A grouping used to organize products and decide stock handling. Chemicals, Equipment, Instruments, Accessories
Quantity Based Items counted by quantity. These appear in Stock Levels. Consumables and accessories
Serialized Items tracked by serial number. These appear in Products/assets. Equipment and instruments
Low Stock Alert Qty The quantity at which the system marks the item as low stock. If alert qty is 5 and stock becomes 5 or less, it is low stock.
PI Purchase Indent. Internal request document before issue/transfer. PI generated when transfer or documented issue is created.
DC Delivery Challan. Dispatch/issue document for material movement. DC generated for approved/dispatched transfer.
Stock Register Register showing stock receipt, issue, transfer, and balance. STR-F-01 Stock Register
Purchase Register Register showing purchase/receipt details from suppliers. PUR-F-07 Purchase Register

The available screens and actions depend on the user's role.

4. Login and Navigation

Logging In

  1. Open the Scientech-IMS application link.
  2. Enter your email address and password.
  3. Click Login.
  4. After login, the Dashboard opens first.

Main Menu

The left sidebar contains the main sections:

  • Dashboard: Summary of stock, alerts, recent movement, and key counts.
  • Products: Add/view equipment, instruments, consumables, and accessories.
  • Stock Levels: Manage quantity-based stock.
  • Categories: Define item categories and stock handling.
  • Transfers: Request, approve, dispatch, and receive stock transfers.
  • Locations: Manage branches, sites, or offices.
  • Compliance: View equipment/instrument calibration and maintenance status.
  • Personnel: Manage field staff and employee records.
  • Audit Logs: Review important system actions.
  • User Management: Add and manage login users.

Search Bar

The top search bar helps search across modules. Use it when you know a product name, transfer number, serial number, or related keyword.

5. Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first place to check the overall health of inventory.

What You Can See

  • Total products and stock summary.
  • Low stock count.
  • Calibration alerts.
  • Recent activity.
  • Location-wise or category-wise overview.

Low Stock Card

Click the low stock card to see which products have reached or gone below the alert quantity.

Low stock is calculated when quantity_on_hand <= low_stock_alert_qty. Example: if DPT Chemical has alert qty 5 and current stock is 5, it is already low stock.

6. Categories

Categories define how products are organized and how they are handled in stock.

When to Create a Category

Create a category when you need a new group of products, such as a chemical group, accessory group, equipment group, or instrument group.

Stock Input / Handling

Handling Use For Where It Appears
Quantity Consumables and accessories counted by quantity. Stock Levels
Serialized Equipment and instruments tracked by serial number. Products / Compliance

Add Category

  1. Go to Categories.
  2. Click Add Category.
  3. Enter the category name.
  4. Select stock handling: Quantity or Serialized.
  5. Add description if needed.
  6. Save.
Do not create duplicate categories for the same purpose. Before adding a new category, search existing categories first.

7. Products

Products are master records. They describe the item, but stock quantity is handled separately for quantity-based items.

Product Types

Type How It Is Tracked Example
Equipment Serialized, one serial number per item. UT machine, yoke, gauge
Instrument Serialized, often calibration controlled. Measuring instrument
Consumable Quantity based. Chemicals, films, cleaners
Accessory Usually quantity based unless configured as serialized. Cables, probes, holders

Add Equipment or Instrument

  1. Go to Products.
  2. Click Add Product.
  3. Select type as equipment or instrument.
  4. Enter name, ID number, serial number, category, location, make, model, and purchase details.
  5. If calibration is required, mark it accordingly.
  6. Save.

Add Consumable or Accessory

  1. Use Stock Levels for normal quantity-based stock creation.
  2. Enter product details and initial location.
  3. Enter opening quantity, even if it is 0.
  4. Enter low stock alert quantity.
Equipment and instruments do not need item count like consumables because each piece is tracked separately by serial number.

8. Stock Levels

Stock Levels is used for consumables and accessories that are tracked by quantity.

Main Buttons

Button Use
Add Stock Item Create a quantity-based product and its initial stock record.
Receive Stock Add purchased stock received from supplier.
Issue Stock Issue quantity-based stock with PI and DC document flow.
Adjust Stock Correct stock quantity for approved reasons.
Send Alert Manually send low-stock email alerts.

Columns to Understand

  • Opening: Initial or opening balance in the report context.
  • Received: Quantity received into stock.
  • Issued: Quantity issued or transferred out.
  • Closing: Current balance.
  • Reserved: Quantity already reserved for transfer requests.
  • Available: Quantity that can still be used.
  • Alert Qty: Low-stock trigger value.
  • Status: OK or Low Stock.

Low Stock Alert Quantity

When adding a new quantity-based item, enter the low stock alert quantity. Admin or Head Office can later change this value using the alert/bell action in Stock Levels.

Example: If DPT Chemical should be reordered when stock reaches 3, set alert qty as 3. The system marks it low stock when current quantity is 3, 2, 1, or 0.

9. Receive Stock

Use Receive Stock when material is purchased from a supplier and physically received.

Steps

  1. Open Stock Levels. Click Receive Stock.
  2. Select location. Choose where the material is received.
  3. Enter supplier and invoice details. These details are used for the Purchase Register.
  4. Add line items. Select item and enter received quantity.
  5. Save receipt. Stock quantity increases at the selected location.
  6. Purchase Register updates. The receipt is available in the purchase register format.

Purchase Register Purpose

Purchase Register is for audit and purchase tracking. It records supplier, invoice number, description, quantity received, type, and remarks.

10. Issue Stock

Use Issue Stock when quantity-based stock is issued for use. The system creates PI and DC documents for audit.

When to Use Issue Stock

  • Issuing consumables or accessories to a site, user, department, or same location work area.
  • Documented local issue inside the same location.
  • Material issue that requires PI and DC reference.

Steps

  1. Go to Stock Levels. Click Issue Stock.
  2. Select item. Choose consumable or accessory.
  3. Select source location. This is where stock will be deducted.
  4. Select destination or same location details. Enter where the material is being used/sent.
  5. Enter quantity. The system checks available stock.
  6. Submit. Stock is issued, and PI/DC references become available.
  7. Print documents if required. Use Print PI and Print DC buttons.
Same-location Issue Stock should not be received again. The system prevents receiving local issue transfers because receiving would add stock back incorrectly.

11. Transfers

Transfers manage material movement from one location to another, or controlled local movement with responsible person details.

Transfer Dashboard

The top cards show Total, Pending, In Transit, Completed, and Rejected transfers.

Views

View Meaning
My Requests Transfers created by you or visible to your role.
For My Action Transfers assigned to you for approval, rejection, or dispatch.

Transfer Status Flow

  1. Requested. Transfer request is created. Stock is reserved at source.
  2. Approved. Manager/admin approves. PI is linked/approved and DC is available.
  3. In Transit. Source dispatches material. Stock is deducted from source.
  4. Awaiting Receipt. Destination has not yet confirmed receipt.
  5. Received. Destination confirms receipt. Stock is added to destination.

Create Transfer Request

  1. Go to Transfers.
  2. Click New Transfer Request.
  3. Select From Location and To Location.
  4. Select responsible person at source.
  5. If same location, select destination responsible person and receiving area/person.
  6. Select transfer type: Consumable, Accessory, Equipment, or Instrument.
  7. Add item and quantity.
  8. Submit.

Approve or Reject

Assigned users can use For My Action to approve or reject transfer requests. Rejection requires a reason.

Dispatch

After approval, click Mark Dispatched or Ship. This deducts stock from source and creates transfer-out movement.

Receive

For inter-location transfers, the destination confirms receipt. This adds stock to the destination and completes the movement.

Equipment and instrument transfers are checked against compliance. If calibration or maintenance is not acceptable, the transfer may be blocked until corrected.

12. PI, DC, Purchase Register, and Stock Register

Purchase Indent (PI)

PI is the internal request document. It is generated automatically for transfer and issue workflows.

PI contains:

  • PI number.
  • Date.
  • From and To information.
  • Item description/specification.
  • Quantity required.
  • Requested by/signature details.

Delivery Challan (DC)

DC is the dispatch/issue document. It is available after approval or dispatch depending on workflow.

DC contains:

  • DC number.
  • Date and reference details.
  • To address/location.
  • Item description.
  • Quantity.
  • Remarks and receiver signature area.

Stock Register

Stock Register follows the STR-F-01 format and tracks opening balance, received quantity, issued/transfer details, and closing balance.

Purchase Register

Purchase Register follows the PUR-F-07 format and records supplier purchase/receipt details for audit.

Generated Document Storage

When PI, DC, purchase register, stock register, or compliance report files are generated, temporary generated copies can be stored for monthly email and download.

Generated files may be deleted after retention, but source data stays permanently in the system. This means reports can be regenerated later.

13. Compliance

Compliance helps verify whether equipment or instruments are fit to transfer/use based on calibration and maintenance status.

Compliance Tabs

Tab Purpose
All Equipment Shows all serialized equipment/instruments and whether they can transfer.
Calibration Shows items with calibration issues or due-soon status.
Maintenance Shows items needing service/maintenance attention.

Status Meaning

  • Clear Item can be transferred.
  • Due Soon Calibration is approaching due date.
  • Blocked Transfer is not allowed until issue is corrected.

Log Calibration

  1. Go to Compliance.
  2. Find the equipment or instrument.
  3. Click Calibration.
  4. Enter calibration date, due date, result, supplier if applicable, and notes.
  5. Save.

Log Maintenance / Service History

  1. Go to Compliance.
  2. Find the equipment or instrument.
  3. Click Service History.
  4. Enter service details and completion information.
  5. Save.

Monthly Compliance CSV

The monthly document email includes a compliance CSV showing current compliance status, blocked reasons, calibration due data, and maintenance status.

16. User Management

User Management is generally used by Admin.

Add User

  1. Go to User Management.
  2. Click Add User.
  3. Enter name, email, password, and role.
  4. Assign location access if needed.

17. Audit Logs

Audit Logs show important actions performed in the system. They help answer who changed what and when.

Use Audit Logs To Check

  • Who created or updated stock.
  • Who approved or rejected a transfer.
  • Who changed user/location/category information.
  • When major actions happened.
Audit logs are for review and traceability. Do not depend on memory or verbal confirmation when an audit log can confirm the action.

18. Monthly Document Emails

The system can prepare monthly generated documents and email them to active Admin email addresses.

Monthly ZIP Includes

  • PI files generated for the month.
  • DC files generated for the month.
  • Purchase Register XLSX.
  • Stock Register XLSX.
  • Compliance Report CSV.

ZIP Splitting

If the monthly ZIP file becomes too large, the system splits it automatically:

  • First attempt: one monthly ZIP.
  • If too large: split into 01-15 and 16-end of month ZIPs.
  • If still too large: split into weekly ZIPs.

Retention

Generated file copies are temporary and can expire after the configured retention period, usually 5 days.

Only generated file copies are deleted. The actual stock, products, transfers, PI data, DC data, calibration, maintenance, and transaction records are not deleted.

19. Low Stock Alerts

Low stock alerts help prevent material shortages.

When Low Stock Is Triggered

The system marks stock as low when:

quantity_on_hand <= low_stock_alert_qty

Automatic Emails

Low-stock emails can be sent automatically when stock falls low and through scheduled checks.

  • After stock adjustment reduces stock.
  • After issue stock.
  • After transfer dispatch.
  • Through scheduled cron checks.

Recipients

  • Location managers assigned to the affected location.
  • Admin and Head Office users for company-wide summary.
  • Additional configured low-stock email addresses, if any.

Manual Send

Go to Stock Levels and click Send Alert to send low-stock emails manually.

20. Daily Workflows

Workflow A: New Consumable Received From Supplier

  1. Create category if not already available.
  2. Add stock item in Stock Levels with low stock alert quantity.
  3. Use Receive Stock when supplier material arrives.
  4. Check Stock Levels to confirm quantity increased.
  5. Export Purchase Register when required.

Workflow B: Issue Consumable to Same Location Lab

  1. Go to Stock Levels.
  2. Click Issue Stock.
  3. Select source location and same destination location.
  4. Enter receiving area/person, such as Lab or Technician name.
  5. Submit issue.
  6. Print PI and DC if needed.

Workflow C: Transfer Material to Another Location

  1. Go to Transfers.
  2. Create New Transfer Request.
  3. Select source and destination locations.
  4. Add items and submit.
  5. Assigned person approves.
  6. Source dispatches material.
  7. Destination receives material.

Workflow D: Equipment Transfer With Compliance

  1. Check Compliance page for the equipment.
  2. If blocked, log calibration or maintenance first.
  3. Create transfer request for serialized item.
  4. Approve and dispatch only after compliance is clear.
  5. Destination receives the item and confirms movement.

Workflow E: Month End Document Preparation

  1. System generates monthly PI/DC/register/compliance files.
  2. System creates one or more ZIP files.
  3. ZIP is emailed to active Admin email addresses.
  4. Generated copies are retained temporarily.
  5. Source records remain available permanently.

21. Troubleshooting

Problem Likely Reason What To Do
Cannot login Wrong password, inactive user, or server issue. Check email/password. Ask Admin to verify user is active.
Location not visible User is not assigned to that location. Ask Admin to update user location access.
Product not showing in Stock Levels It may be serialized equipment/instrument or no stock row exists. Check product type/category. Add stock item or initial location record.
Transfer approval not allowed User lacks permission, assignment, or location access. Check role, assigned person, and location access.
Equipment transfer blocked Calibration or maintenance issue exists. Open Compliance page and resolve the listed issue.
Low stock email not received Email settings, recipient setup, or cron setup may be missing. Check email configuration, user emails, location managers, and cron secret.
Monthly ZIP not received Admin email missing, email disabled, or cron not configured. Check active Admin email, SMTP settings, and monthly cron setup.
Stock quantity looks wrong Receipt, issue, transfer, or adjustment may have been entered incorrectly. Review Stock Register, transactions, and audit logs.

Before Asking for Support

  1. Note the screen name.
  2. Note the item name/SKU/serial number.
  3. Note the transfer number, PI number, or DC number if available.
  4. Take a screenshot of the error or unexpected value.
  5. Check Audit Logs if it is related to a change.

22. Best Practices

For Stock Users

  • Always select the correct location before receiving or issuing stock.
  • Enter invoice and supplier details during receipt.
  • Do not use adjustment for normal issue or receipt. Use the proper workflow.
  • Set low stock alert quantity when creating new stock items.

For Transfer Users

  • Use clear notes when requesting material.
  • Approve only after checking source and destination information.
  • Dispatch only after material physically leaves source.
  • Receive only after material physically reaches destination.

For Compliance Users

  • Log calibration as soon as certificate is received.
  • Update maintenance/service status promptly.
  • Check compliance before planning equipment movement.

For Admin

  • Keep user roles and location assignments accurate.
  • Make sure admin email is correct for monthly document emails.
  • Keep SMTP and cron secrets configured in deployment.
  • Review audit logs regularly.
Good data entry is the most important part of the system. If receipt, issue, transfer, and calibration entries are correct, reports and audit documents will also be correct.
